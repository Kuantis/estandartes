Estandartes

Real-time strategy for your phone.

Build your village, gather resources, raise your banner and defend your Base. Play as Rome, Egypt or the Mongols against a rival that thinks, scouts and attacks.

Coming soon to Google Play For Android 7.1 or later. The game is in Spanish.
  • Free
  • Works offline
  • No accounts
  • 1 vs 1 over WiFi

The game

A complete strategy match in the palm of your hand: economy, building, army and defense, with touch controls designed for your thumb.

⚔️

Three civilizations

Rome holds firm with infantry and sturdy buildings, Egypt gathers and builds faster, and the Mongols strike with their cavalry. Each has a unique unit: the Legionary, the War Chariot and the Rider.

🧠

A rival that thinks

The computer scouts, expands, gathers strength before attacking and even surrenders when it sees it is losing. Each civilization plays with its own personality, on Easy, Normal or Hard.

🌙

Fog, day and night

The map starts hidden. Send your Scout, build watchtowers and beware of the night: you see less, and the rival's Scout can sneak up on you.

🏰

Defend your Base

Draw walls with your finger, open gates, build towers and station guards in your watchtowers. Under attack? Shelter your villagers inside the Base.

📶

1 vs 1 over WiFi

Challenge a friend on the same WiFi network or a phone's hotspot. No internet, no servers and no accounts: just a room code and a password.

🗺️

Your maps, your banner

Paint your own map, test it and share it. Pick your team color from a colorblind-friendly palette, and your banner for every match.

Your privacy, in short

  • We don't ask you to create an account and we don't collect your personal data.
  • Your saved games, settings and maps stay on your phone only.
  • Ads are child-appropriate and non-personalized, and never appear while you play. No purchases and no analytics.
  • 1 vs 1 connects the phones directly over your WiFi, with no servers.

All the details are in the privacy policy and the terms of use.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to play?

No. The whole game works offline. For 1 vs 1 you only need both phones on the same WiFi network, or one phone sharing its hotspot; no mobile data or internet is required.

Is it free? Does it have ads?

Yes, it's free. From version 0.7.1 it has child-appropriate, non-personalized ads: in the shop, if you want, you watch 1 to 3 to win resources or soldiers; the main menu has a banner, and sometimes a full-screen ad appears when you return to the menu. They never appear while you play. Later we plan a one-time purchase to remove them. Before we do, we will update the privacy policy and the terms, and let you know.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.1 or later. The game adapts to the power of each phone.

Where are my saved games? How do I delete them?

Only on your phone. They are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data in Settings › Apps › Estandartes › Storage › Clear data. We don't have any copy.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. It's a strategy game with battles between ancient armies, and it doesn't collect data from anyone, including children. For 1 vs 1, we recommend playing with people you know and on trusted networks.

I found a bug or have an idea. Where do I send it?

Write to us at kuantis.dev@gmail.com. If you can, include your phone model and the game version, shown at the bottom right of the main menu.
